# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback

Examples of behavior that harms the environment include:

- Harassment, bullying, or discrimination
- Harmful or threatening language
- Disruption of community activities or conversations

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and real-time communication).

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common community standards and designed to be practical for open-source projects.
